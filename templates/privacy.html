Privacy policy

BRIO (brio.org.in) is a video-editing service. This page explains what we collect, why, and who processes it on our behalf.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and sign-in information.
  • Project details and the files you upload for editing, along with edited versions we deliver.
  • Order and payment records, such as amounts, status and payment references. We do not store card or bank details.
  • Messages and activity inside your workspace, kept for security and support.

How we use it

Only to run the service: to create and edit your projects, take payment, deliver your files, send you service emails (for example, payment reminders and project updates) and keep accounts secure. We do not sell your data or use your files for advertising.

Who processes it for us

  • Amazon Web Services: private storage of uploaded and delivered files.
  • Supabase: our database.
  • Vercel: website hosting.
  • Razorpay: payment processing.
  • Google (Gmail): sending service emails from brio4edits@gmail.com.

Who can see your files

Your files are never public. Only you, BRIO administrators and the editor assigned to your project can open them, through short-lived private links.

How long we keep it

Projects left unpaid are deleted, with every uploaded file, 36 hours after they are created. You can ask us to delete your account or other data at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email brio4edits@gmail.com.
